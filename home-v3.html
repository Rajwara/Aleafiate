Our practitioners may support patients experiencing:

Pain & Physical discomfort

Ongoing pain can affect your sleep, work and mood. Our practitioners can talk with you about:

  • Ongoing pain
  • Joint pain or stiffness
  • Muscle tension
  • Back discomfort
  • Headaches

Sleep & Rest

Poor sleep can affect your energy, mood, and overall wellbeing. Our practitioners can help you explore options for improving your sleep.

Hover to see more

Sleep & Rest

Sleep shapes how you feel every day. Our practitioners can talk with you about:

  • Trouble falling asleep
  • Waking during the night
  • Restless or light sleep
  • Difficulty staying asleep
  • Waking unrefreshed

Mental & Emotional Wellbeing

Mental wellbeing is an important part of your overall health. Our practitioners are here to support you and discuss your concerns.

Hover to see more

Mental & Emotional Wellbeing

It can be hard to ask for help — we offer a calm, non-judgemental space to talk about:

  • Low mood
  • Feeling on edge
  • Feeling overwhelmed
  • Difficulty relaxing
  • Loss of interest in usual activities

Women’s Health

Women’s health needs can change throughout different stages of life. Our practitioners can help you manage your symptoms.

Hover to see more

Women’s Health

We take women’s health seriously and plan care around your stage of life, including:

  • Menstrual pain & cramping
  • Menopause symptoms
  • Changes in mood or sleep
  • Changes in appetite
