Privacy and Patient Information

How Aleafiate Clinics collects, uses, protects and shares your personal and health information.

01

Collection and Use of Personal Information

  • Intake Form Submission: Aleafiate Clinics collects relevant personal and health information through an Intake Form before appointments to help healthcare practitioners understand each patient’s individual healthcare needs. This information may include medical history, current health information, and insurance details.
  • Additional Documentation: Additional documentation, such as health summaries or other relevant supporting information, may be requested when required to support the patient’s clinical assessment and ongoing care.
02

Information Protection and Confidentiality

Aleafiate Clinics is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal information. Patient information is stored securely, with access limited to authorised personnel.

For more information about how personal information is collected, used, and handled, please refer to the Privacy Policy available on our website or request a copy.

03

Information Sharing with Third Parties

Aleafiate Clinics may share relevant patient information with trusted third-party service providers, such as HotDoc, where necessary to facilitate telehealth appointments. Third-party service providers are required to maintain appropriate privacy and confidentiality standards.

04

Retention and Disposal of Information

Aleafiate Clinics retains patient records as required for ongoing care, legal compliance, and operational purposes. Records are securely stored and disposed of in accordance with applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

05

Patient Rights

Patients may request access to their records, request corrections to inaccurate information, or obtain a copy of the Aleafiate Clinics Privacy Policy by contacting the clinic directly.

Questions about your information?
