Why regular follow-up consultations matter

Your first consultation is only the beginning. Regular follow-ups are how your practitioner keeps your care safe, effective and right for you as things change.

Care that adapts to you

Health rarely stays the same. Your symptoms, routine, other medicines and goals can all change over time. Follow-up consultations give your practitioner the chance to see how you’re going and adjust your plan so it keeps working for you.

What happens at a follow-up?

  • A review of your progress since your last appointment.
  • A conversation about anything that’s changed — your health, lifestyle or other treatments.
  • Adjustments to your plan, if needed.
  • Prescription renewals, where clinically appropriate.

Follow-ups can be by telehealth or in clinic, and they’re usually shorter than your initial consultation. They cost $65 each, and you’ll always know the cost before you book.

Safety first: monitoring progress is an essential part of safe treatment. That’s why attending regular follow-ups, as your practitioner directs, is part of our terms and conditions.

Tell us about side effects early

If something doesn’t feel right between appointments, don’t wait for your next follow-up. Contact us on 07 3473 3396 or admin@aleafiate.com.au, and report any side effects to your practitioner promptly.

Getting the most from your follow-up

  1. Keep brief notes on how you’ve been feeling between visits.
  2. Let us know about any new medicines, including ones from other doctors.
  3. Bring your questions — follow-ups are a two-way conversation.
  4. Book your next appointment before you finish, so there’s no gap in your care.

This article is general information only and isn’t medical advice. For advice about your own health, please speak with a registered healthcare professional.
