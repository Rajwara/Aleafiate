Our mission

Patient-focused care, every step of the way.

At Aleafiate Health Clinics, we believe healing is a shared journey. Three promises guide every decision our team makes.

We listen first

Every consultation begins with understanding your health concerns, medical history and individual needs. Nothing is rushed, and there’s no pressure to make a decision on the spot.

Honest, independent advice

Every patient is assessed independently by an AHPRA-registered practitioner. If an option isn’t right for you, we’ll tell you, and explain which other options may suit you better.

Support that continues

We plan for the long term. Regular follow-ups mean your care plan is reviewed and adjusted as your health changes, with our care team on hand between visits.

Care that fits your life

Choose the care that suits you, with face-to-face appointments at our Logan Central clinic and telehealth consultations available Australia-wide.

Our approach

Four steps, at your pace.

From your first conversation to ongoing care, here’s how we work with you.

01
Step 01

Listen

It starts with a free, no-obligation nurse call. Tell us about your health, your history and what you’d like help with, at your own pace.

02
Step 02

Assess

Complete your intake form, then meet an AHPRA-registered practitioner by telehealth or in clinic for a thorough assessment.

03
Step 03

Plan

Your practitioner discusses suitable care options and, where clinically appropriate, creates a personalised plan around your health needs.

04
Step 04

Follow up

Regular follow-up consultations let your practitioner review your progress and adjust your plan as needed.
