What to expect at your first telehealth consultation

Seeing a practitioner from home is simple once you know how it works. Here’s how to get ready, what happens during the call, and what comes next.

Why telehealth?

Telehealth lets you see an AHPRA-registered practitioner by video without travelling to the clinic. It’s a good fit if you live outside Logan, work long hours, find travel difficult, or simply feel more comfortable talking from home. You get the same practitioner and the same standard of care as an in-clinic visit at Logan Central Plaza.

Before your appointment

  • Complete your intake form. It covers your medical history and current health, so your practitioner has the full picture before you meet. Please submit it in good time, as appointments may be cancelled if the form isn’t completed.
  • Check your device. A phone, tablet or laptop with a camera, microphone and a reliable internet connection is all you need. Charge it beforehand.
  • Find a quiet, private space. Somewhere you can talk openly without being overheard or interrupted.
  • Have your details ready. A list of any medicines you take, recent test results or letters from other doctors, and your preferred pharmacy.

During the consultation

Your practitioner will talk through your health history, your current concerns and what you’d like help with. They may ask follow-up questions about your intake form, past treatments and how your symptoms affect daily life.

This is also your time to ask questions. Nothing is rushed, and there’s no pressure to make a decision on the spot.

Good to know: every clinical decision is made independently by your practitioner, in line with Australian law. If a treatment isn’t safe or suitable for you, they’ll explain why and talk you through other options.

After the call

If treatment is appropriate, your practitioner will put a personalised plan in place. Prescriptions are sent directly to your chosen pharmacy within 12–24 hours, along with any required TGA approvals. Payment is processed after the consultation, and scripts are released once payment is complete.

You’ll then book regular follow-ups, so your practitioner can check how you’re going and adjust your plan as needed.

Tips for a smooth call

  1. Join a few minutes early to test your sound and video.
  2. Sit facing a window or lamp so your practitioner can see you clearly.
  3. Write your questions down beforehand so nothing is forgotten.
  4. Keep a pen and paper nearby for any notes.

This article is general information only and isn’t medical advice. For advice about your own health, please speak with a registered healthcare professional.
