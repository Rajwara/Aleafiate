How it works

Safe, simple and never rushed.

Every step follows TGA and AHPRA requirements, and your practitioner explains everything along the way.

Read the FAQs
01Step 01

Free call & intake form

Start with a free, no-obligation call. Then complete our intake form so your practitioner has your medical history before you meet.

02Step 02

Consultation

Meet your practitioner by telehealth or at our Logan Central clinic. They assess your health and check whether a treatment is safe and suitable for you.

03Step 03

Your plan

If treatment is appropriate, your practitioner creates a personalised plan. Prescriptions are sent to your chosen pharmacy within 12–24 hours, with any required TGA approvals.

04Step 04

Follow-up care

Regular follow-up consultations let your practitioner review your progress and adjust your plan as needed.
