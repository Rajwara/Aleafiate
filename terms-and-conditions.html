Patient Information

Terms and Conditions

Please read the following terms carefully before booking or attending a consultation with Aleafiate Clinics.

01

Telehealth Appointment Consent

By scheduling a telehealth appointment with Aleafiate Clinics, you consent to participate in a remote medical consultation with one of our qualified healthcare practitioners.

Before your appointment, please complete the Intake Form with your relevant medical history and health information. If the form is not completed before your appointment, your consultation may need to be rescheduled or cancelled.

02

Understanding Alternative Healthcare

  • Individualised Care: Alternative healthcare begins with understanding each patient’s individual healthcare needs, medical history, current concerns, and existing care.
  • Doctor-Led Consultation: During the consultation, the doctor will discuss the patient’s healthcare needs and explain care options that may be appropriate for their individual circumstances.
  • Health Information: Patients should provide accurate information about their medical history, current medications, and other relevant health information to support an informed clinical assessment.
03

Follow-Up Consultations

Patients may be asked to attend follow-up consultations as recommended by their healthcare provider. These consultations allow the doctor to review progress, discuss any concerns, and provide ongoing care based on individual healthcare needs.

04

Post-Consultation Process

Following the consultation, the doctor will discuss the next steps based on the patient’s individual healthcare needs and clinical assessment. Any care recommendations will be determined by the doctor based on the patient’s medical history, current circumstances, and what is clinically appropriate.

05

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Aleafiate Clinics has a zero-tolerance policy for abusive or aggressive behaviour towards staff and healthcare practitioners. Such behaviour may result in the termination of services.

06

Rescheduling Policy

Patients must provide at least 24 hours’ notice if they need to reschedule an appointment. This notice enables us to offer the appointment time to other patients.

07

Agreement to Terms and Policies

By using Aleafiate Clinics’ services, patients agree to the current Aleafiate Terms and Conditions and applicable Privacy Policies.

08

Payment Policy

Pre-authorisation of payment details (credit or debit card) is required before the consultation.

Any applicable consultation fees will be processed in accordance with Aleafiate Clinics’ payment terms.
